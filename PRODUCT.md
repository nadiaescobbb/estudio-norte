# Estudio Norte

register: brand

## Product Purpose

Conceptual website for Trama Studio showing how a construction company can use a website as a commercial trust tool, not as a generic brochure.

The site must demonstrate that Trama understands the construction buyer: people want clarity before investing, a visible process, realistic claims, and a simple path to ask for an evaluation.

## Users

- Homeowners planning to build, expand, or renovate.
- Shops and small businesses needing commercial renovations.
- SMEs with industrial or logistics construction needs.
- Leads who already have drawings, budgets, photos, or a half-started build and need a technical opinion.

## Brand Voice

Calm, concrete, procedural. It should feel like a site made by people who know job sites, budgets, and decision fatigue.

Avoid hype, impossible guarantees, fake authority, fake metrics, and template construction language.

## Strategic Principles

- Sell confidence through process, not perfection.
- Make services understandable before the first call.
- Replace absolute promises with visible controls, stages, and decisions.
- Treat the contact form as a qualifier for real sales conversations.
- Present the case honestly as conceptual work by Trama Studio.

## Anti References

- "Empresa lider" generic construction copy.
- Pricing by square meter as a universal product.
- "0.00% deviation", guaranteed deadlines, or perfect outcomes.
- Fake badges, fake system labels, and overly technical theatre.
- Beige editorial sameness without construction-specific evidence.
